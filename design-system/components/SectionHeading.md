# SectionHeading

Section opener: eyebrow, a two-line headline, and an intro paragraph in the right column.

**Consumer provides:** `title` (put a `<br/>` between the two short statements), optional `eyebrow`/`index`, `intro`. `size="display"` only for the hero and the closing "Was hast du vor?". Stacks to one column under 720px container width and steps the type down to the mobile sizes.

Headlines are statements ending in a period: "Drei Disziplinen. Ein klarer Anspruch." Never a question except the final CTA.
