# ServiceCard

One discipline: 4:3 visual, mono index, title with arrow, short copy, tag line.

**Consumer provides:** `index`, `title`, `text`, `tags`, a `visual` (illustration — never a client screenshot or a claimed result), optional `href` (the whole card becomes the link target, focus ring around the card).

Use in a 3-column grid on desktop (`space-6` gap), one column on mobile. Keep copy to ≤ 2 sentences. `visualTone="inverse"` for at most one card per row.
